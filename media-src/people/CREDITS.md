# Photographs

Free photographs from Pexels, used under its licence (commercial use allowed, no attribution
required; credited here anyway), downloaded at 2400 px wide. In the photos `scripts/compose.mjs`
lists as composites, the iPhone's screen is replaced with a real Wealthit capture, fitted to the
phone's angle; the rest are shown as photographed. The people shown are not Wealthit users and the site never says they are (the Pexels licence: do
not imply endorsement). The owner's rules: people from many places, every woman shown wears a
hijab, and a new photograph is shown to the owner, composited, before it goes on the site. The
originals are not committed (the repo is public): to run the script again, download each from its
link below at 2400 px wide into this folder, under the name given.
